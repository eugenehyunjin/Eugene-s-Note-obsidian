1. 태종대
Located at the southernmost point of about 9.1km along the Yeongdo coast after passing the Busan Bridge.
Taejongdae Amusement Park has an area of 1,632,809㎡ and is densely packed with 120 kinds of trees, including sea pine, 
It has been visited by many domestic and foreign tourists.
you can see the cliffs, strange rock formations, and the open Korean Strait.

On a clear day, you can see Tsushima Island, Japan, which is about 56 kilometers away
and it has long been a popular tourist attraction for citizens and visitors.

It has been used as a military fortress for a long time since Japanese colonial rule.

but in 1967, the Ministry of Construction and Transportation announced it as an amusement park.

On December 2013, it was recognized as a national geopark.

3. 흰여울 마을
Before the Korean War, the population of Busan was only 250,000 to 300,000 people. After the war, refugees fled to Busan, and the population grew to 10 million.

The war continued until 1953. In July 1953, North Korea signed an armistice with South Korea. Korea is an armistice country.  
Where are the 10 million people? After the war, most of the refugees returned to their hometowns, but many remained. Because of poverty, the people did not have enough money to build a house. And you'll notice how mountainous the area of Busan is. There is very little flat land and houses can only be built on hillsides or coastal areas. That's why we have two villages that we go to today. White Shoal Culture Village and Gamcheon Culture Village.

To learn more about life after the Korean War and the Korean War, I recommend you to watch two movies, the first one is "Highland War", which is about the Korean War. The second movie, "International Market," tells the story of a family living in Busan after the Korean War. Both films are very good.

4. 감천 문화마을
It is a Gamcheon culture village called Machu Picchu (马丘比丘) in Korea. When the Korean War broke out in 1950, refugees from the upper part of the country flocked to Busan, and they had to build a new village. The city was already in full demand, so they cut down the mountain and built the village. That's why Busan has a lot of villiges on mountains.
